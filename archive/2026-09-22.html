Where we stand

Monitoring is established. There is not yet enough repeat observation to judge whether this particular local market is weakening. The initial panel contains one core-area property and four wider-area candidates; it is a watchlist, not a representative market sample.

Nickle Cottages is the current Chartham lead. Its guide is £250,000–£275,000; the road setting needs investigation. No price reduction has been verified. Other homes below were previously shortlisted, not newly listed today.

The buying brief

Purchase ceiling £275,000, preferably £230,000–£250,000 or below; at least two usable bedrooms; privacy, garden or courtyard, character and manageable condition. Core research covers Chartham, Chartham Hatch, Chilham, Old Wives Lees, Selling and nearby Canterbury villages. The wider opportunity search covers locations within about two hours’ drive of Chilham, with individual routes checked before new recommendations.

Core comparison research will also include 2–3-bedroom freehold homes at £175,000–£350,000. Higher-priced comparables are not purchase recommendations.

What we will learn over time

  • Supply: new listings, older unsold stock, price bands and usable alternatives.
  • Demand: observed sales-agreed transitions, time on market and failed sales returning to market.
  • Pricing: same-property reductions and comparable completed sales, keeping asking and achieved prices separate.
  • Ownership: condition, EPC, noise, garden privacy, access rights, flood exposure and likely costs.
  • Broader conditions: mortgage costs, inflation, employment, rents and local planning changes when fresh evidence becomes available.

Decision rules

Evaluate discounts against supported comparable value, not the original asking price. Look for repeated reductions or long marketing periods, then investigate the seller’s circumstances and property condition. Keep survey and legal checks. A home should remain affordable and suitable through a hypothetical 20% price fall and a longer holding period.

How this report updates

The daily scheduled task refreshes the local report and retains dated editions. Meaningful opportunities are flagged in the task; a Monday digest summarises accumulated evidence. Daily silence means no alert-worthy change, not that every listing has been verified.
