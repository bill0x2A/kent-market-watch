Purchase ceiling £275,000, preferably £230,000–£250,000 or below; two usable bedrooms; privacy, garden/courtyard, character and manageable condition. Core: Chartham, Chartham Hatch, Chilham, Old Wives Lees, Selling and surrounding Canterbury villages. Wider discovery: individually checked routes within approximately two hours of Chilham.
